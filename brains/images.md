# Rules for every image

These rules are added to every image Jillian makes (social posts and family cards). Google's Nano Banana model reads them, so keep them short and plain. Google applies its own content rules on top and may refuse an image.

Parks, castles, ships, rides and characters are all fine in the picture. The rules below are the only limits.

## Branding
- Don't copy official logos or wordmarks (Disney, Universal, cruise lines, resorts) onto the image, and don't make it look like an official Disney or Universal ad.
- A character can appear in a scene, but never as Jillian's own logo, mascot or brand mark, and no character names in the image's words.
- Nothing may say or suggest that Jillian works for Disney or Universal.

## Claims
- No prices, discounts, dates or promotion details unless they're in her direction, word for word.
- No client names on images for posts. A family card may use the family's first names if her direction gives them.

## Style
- Warm, bright, joyful and family-friendly.

- Leave a little space around the edges so the image can be cropped for each app.

## Words on the image
- Only add words when her direction asks for them, or for a card's greeting ("30 days to go!", "Welcome home!").
- Keep it to one short line (8 words or fewer), spelled exactly as given, in a clean, easy-to-read font.
- Never add the legal footer to the image; it goes in the post's caption.

## Her photos
- When she includes her own photos, the people in them must still look like themselves: the same faces, ages, skin and bodies. Don't add or remove people unless she asks.
- Use her photos as she describes: as the scene, the style, or the people to include.
