# Fit rules for new inquiries

Jillian's own rules for deciding whether an inquiry is a good fit. The Welcome Center checks inquiries against these, and only these.

**Status: first draft, from John's notes in the 10/31/2026 review. Jillian to confirm the numbers.** Use every section that's filled in. Where a section an inquiry needs still says TODO, say "no rule yet" for that part instead of guessing.

## How Jillian is paid
- Suppliers pay a commission of about **10%** of the trip cost. All-inclusive resorts often pay a little more.
- MMV keeps about 20–30% of that, so Jillian keeps about **70–80%**. Example: a $10,000 trip pays about $1,000, of which Jillian keeps about $700–800.
- Families usually pay Jillian nothing; she's paid through the commission.

## Is it worth her time?
- A full trip usually takes Jillian **5–10 hours** of planning (less with this assistant).
- Rule of thumb: a trip **under about $5,000** usually isn't worth a full planning effort, since her share would be about $350 or less. Friends and family are the exception.
- For anything under that, or with no stated budget, recommend the **Quick ideas** reply. Recommend **Let's plan it** for families who are ready to book and whose trip clears the bar.

## Lead time
- The families worth a full plan are **ready to book within about 2 weeks**.
- The Blueprint Call normally happens **within about a week** of the inquiry.
- Window-shoppers who only want information, with no plan to book soon, get the **Quick ideas** reply.

## Destinations I book / don't book
- **Book:** Walt Disney World and Disneyland; Universal Orlando; cruises (mostly Royal Caribbean and Disney Cruise Line, but any line, including Viking); all-inclusive resorts such as Sandals and Beaches; Adventures by Disney.
- **Anything else:** case by case. She prefers fewer all-inclusives, though requests are growing.
