# Jillian's know-how

Tips Jillian has given clients in her own emails (November 2026). They're the kind of practical detail clients love her for, so use them in plans, replies and tips where they fit.

**Every tip here is "verify".** Parks and cruise lines change rules, perks, hours and locations often. Say "verify before quoting" in the notes for Jillian when you rely on one, and never present one as a guarantee. None of this is a price: never quote a price from here or from her sample emails.

## Universal Orlando
- Unlimited Express hotels and which parks Express covers: see the Universal notes in the booking-windows sheet.
- Portofino Bay has the most room of the three Signature hotels (about 450 sq. ft.). All three have rooms with 2 queen beds, and a rollaway can be added for a nightly fee.
- Getting around: from Portofino Bay and Hard Rock, water taxis go to Universal Studios Florida and Islands of Adventure, and shuttles go to Epic Universe and Volcano Bay. Portofino Bay is about a 15-minute walk to CityWalk; Hard Rock is the closest of the three to the two parks and CityWalk.
- Portofino Bay has 3 pools and Italian-style dining (including a gelateria and café). Hard Rock's pool plays music underwater.
- Extras to remind families about in a quote: food and drink, souvenirs, flights, transfers or Uber, a rollaway, hotel parking (nightly fee) and Cancel For Any Reason coverage.

## Disney Cruise Line
- Documents: each child needs a passport or an original birth certificate (raised seal, not hospital-issued, no copies). Passports should be valid for 6 months after the trip and have no damage.
- After booking: create the DCL account with the same email and password as the family's My Disney Experience account, and link a Walt Disney World package and a cruise package in the online accounts.
- Flights home on debarkation day: no earlier than noon, to allow for traffic, delays, airport crowds and transfers.

## Disneyland Resort
- PhotoPass comes with Lightning Lane tickets in her packages. The family's PhotoPass code is in the Disneyland app: Menu (three lines) › Photos › Link Photos › Show PhotoPass Code. A screenshot of the code saves reloading the app for every photographer. Ride photos have to be linked by hand after the ride (tip: take a picture of it, then link it).
- Rope drop: the main security checkpoints in Downtown Disney open about an hour before park opening, and the gates usually open about 30 minutes before. Arriving around 7:00 for an 8:00 opening is about right. Ropes are set up around the central hub by the castle, and drop at opening time.
- Disney California Adventure for Pixar Place Hotel guests: the hotel's own entrance (near the Grand Californian) opens around 7:30, and guests wait near Corn Dog Castle until rope drop. Use it to head first to Pixar Pier or Paradise Gardens Park (Incredicoaster, Toy Story Midway Mania). Use the Main Gate in Downtown Disney to head first to Cars Land (Radiator Springs Racers) or Hollywood Land (Guardians of the Galaxy – Mission: BREAKOUT!).
- Groceries (Instacart) to Pixar Place: deliveries go to Bell Services, who hold them or bring them up (a fee or tip may apply). In the delivery notes, put the lead guest's name, the check-in date and the reservation confirmation number, and ask the driver to leave it with Bell Services. Alcohol needs the guest there to receive it.
- Bags: anything bigger than 24" long × 15" wide × 18" high isn't allowed in the parks, and neither is loose or dry ice (reusable ice packs are fine).
- Shopping without carrying bags: shops don't deliver to rooms, but there's a free merchandise hold at Pioneer Mercantile (Frontierland) and The Star Trader (Tomorrowland) in Disneyland, and at Elias & Co. (Buena Vista Street), Sarge's Surplus Hut (Cars Land) and Avengers Vault (Avengers Campus) in California Adventure. The Stroller Shop in the Esplanade holds purchases too. Lockers (fee) are in the Esplanade, to the left of each park's entrance, and inside both parks.
- Lightning Lane: Multi Pass may already be in the package with the tickets, and a resort stay has included one free Multi Pass, available after 7:00 on check-in day. Single Pass is always extra. The ePackage arrives a few weeks before the trip; that's when tickets get linked in the app.
- Restaurant reservations made outside Disney (for example on Yelp for Downtown Disney) only need that confirmation.
- Suggest a quick call with the family after their ePackage arrives, to go over Lightning Lane and the app.
