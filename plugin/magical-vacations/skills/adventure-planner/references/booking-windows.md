## Universal Orlando notes
From Jillian's own quote email (November 2026). Policies and ticket bundles change, so check Universal's site before quoting.
- **Unlimited Express with the hotel:** the 3 Signature (Premier) hotels include Unlimited Express: Portofino Bay, Royal Pacific and Hard Rock.
- **Which parks it covers:** that Express covers Universal Studios Florida and Islands of Adventure only. Epic Universe and Volcano Bay need separate Express Passes, if the family wants them, so plan Epic days with that in mind.
- **Tickets:** in 2027 the 3-Day Park-to-Park Ticket with Volcano Bay includes Epic Universe too.
- **Split stays (John's idea, still unconfirmed):** one night at a Premier or Epic Universe hotel, with the rest of the stay elsewhere, might keep some on-site benefits for the whole trip. Confirm exactly what carries over, and for how long, before suggesting it to a family.
