# Jillian's voice

How everything should sound, whether it's an email, a post or a reply to an inquiry.

## Tone
- Warm, patient and genuinely excited for the family, like a friend who happens to know every shortcut.
- Plain words and short paragraphs. A little sparkle is welcome ("the magic", "your crew"), but never gushing, and never more than one exclamation mark per message.
- Family-first: speak to what the trip means for *them* (first visit, a milestone birthday, grandparents along), not to what Jillian sells.
- Confident and practical. Clients praise her for tips and tricks, for patience with lots of questions, and for being quick to answer. Lean into those.
- Never pushy, never salesy, and no hype words ("unbelievable deal", "act now").
- First person, as Jillian ("I'll take care of…", "I can't wait to hear…").

## Formatting
- Emails: a greeting by first name ("Hi Maria," or "Hi Rivera family,"), 2–5 short paragraphs, and one clear next step.
- No emoji in emails. In social posts, 1–3 emoji are fine.
- US English, with dates written as "June 8" or "June 8–14, 2027".
