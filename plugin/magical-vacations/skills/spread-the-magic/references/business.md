# Jillian's business

## Who she is
Jillian Jochum plans family vacations as an independent contractor affiliated with Magical Moments Vacations (MMV), an Authorized Disney Vacation Planner. She works one-on-one with each family, from the first idea to the "welcome home."

Website: https://www.magicalvacationsbyjillian.com/
Email: jillian.jochum@mmvagent.com
Facebook: facebook.com/MMVbyJillian
Instagram: @mmvbyjillian

## Where she travels
- **Walt Disney World and Disneyland:** family adventures across the parks.
- **Universal Orlando:** Universal Studios Florida, Islands of Adventure, Epic Universe, Volcano Bay and the rest of the resort.
- **Cruises:** mostly Royal Caribbean and Disney Cruise Line; she books any cruise line, including Viking.
- **All-inclusive resorts:** for example Sandals and Beaches. She prefers fewer of these, but requests are growing as friends' kids get older, and the commission is good.
- **Adventures by Disney:** guided all-inclusive trips around the world. Occasional requests.
- **Anything else** is case by case.

## Planning tiers (wording from her website)
- **Magical Vacations:** for families who want a seamless experience. Jillian handles the heavy lifting, from booking tickets to arranging transportation.
- **Concierge:** for families who want a truly personalized touch, with 24/7 support and custom itinerary design.

## How new clients start
1. The family fills out the "Plan Now" form on her website, sharing their crew, dream destinations and realistic budget.
2. If it looks like a good fit, Jillian invites them to a complimentary **Vacation Blueprint Call** to narrow down dates, party size and resort matches.
3. After the call she builds side-by-side package options in Travefy for them to review.
