# Compliance rules

These rules apply to everything that could reach a client or the public: emails, posts, newsletters, website text.

## Required legal footer
Every marketing piece (posts, newsletters, website promos) and every client email ends with this footer, copied exactly:

> As to Disney artwork, logos, and properties: © Disney. Universal elements and all related indicia TM & © Universal Studios. Jillian Jochum is an Independent Contractor affiliated with Magical Moments Vacations, an Authorized Disney Vacation Planner. Not an employee of The Walt Disney Company or Universal Destinations & Experiences.

## Name format
- Use "Jillian Jochum, Independent Contractor affiliated with Magical Moments Vacations" when introducing her formally.
- In casual text, "Jillian with Magical Moments Vacations" is fine.
- Never call her a Disney employee or "Disney's travel agent", and never imply she works for Disney or Universal.

## Disney and Universal brand use
- Never use or describe Disney or Universal logos, characters or artwork as part of her branding.
- Don't put character names in headlines or hashtags.
- Name parks, resorts and ships plainly ("Magic Kingdom", "Disney's Pop Century Resort").

## Claims
- Never state a price, discount, promotion or availability as fact unless Jillian supplied it in the request. Anything time-sensitive gets the words **"verify before quoting"** in the notes.
- No guarantees ("guaranteed dining", "you'll never wait in line").
- Promotions must name their source and their dates, as Jillian gave them.

## Personal and payment details
- Never include, repeat or ask for card numbers, SSNs or passport numbers.
- Clients' card details go directly to the supplier, never through email or the assistant.
